🌴 PART 1: OAHU (City & Surf)

Transport: Uber/Bus + 1 Day Rental. Stay: Waikiki Banyan.

Day 1 (Feb 9): Landing & Waikiki Arrival
📅 The Plan
  • Arrive in Honolulu (HNL) at 3:55 PM. UberXL to Condo.
  • 3:00 PM onwards: Check-in to the Waikiki Banyan.
  • Walk Waikiki Strip. Take a photo with the Duke Kahanamoku statue.
  • Grocery Run (Safeway Kapahulu or Instacart delivery).
🍳 Menu
  • Dinner (The Big Meal Out): Marukame Udon (Handmade noodles, very affordable).
🍍
Daily Treat: Dole Whip at Island Vintage Shave Ice.
Day 2 (Feb 10): Pearl Harbor History
📅 The Plan
  • Take TheBus (#20 or #42) to Pearl Harbor.
  • Core: USS Arizona Memorial.
  • Bonus: Battleship Missouri tickets for history buffs ($$).
  • Evening: Free Hula Show at Kūhiō Beach at sunset.
🍳 Menu
  • Lunch: Packed Snacks/Sandwiches (eat on the lawn outside the gates).
  • Dinner (Condo): Spaghetti Night.
🥞
Daily Treat: Leonard's Bakery Malasadas.
Book Arizona Tickets ($1 fee)
Day 3 (Feb 11): North Shore Loop 1-Day Car Rental
📅 The Plan
  • Morning Option: Shark’s Cove (Snorkel) OR Waimea Valley Botanical Garden (Walk/Swim).
  • Lunch: North Shore Food Trucks (e.g., Giovanni's Shrimp).
  • Afternoon: Dole Plantation Maze & Train.
  • Scenic Drive: Optional return via East Coast to see the Halona Blowhole.
🍳 Menu
  • Lunch: Food Trucks (Eating Out).
  • Dinner: Leftovers at the condo.
🍧
Daily Treat: Matsumoto Shave Ice in Haleiwa.
Day 4 (Feb 12): Hike & Relax Nature
📅 The Plan
  • Hike Diamond Head early morning.
  • Afternoon beach swim in Waikiki.
🍳 Menu
  • Lunch: Sandwiches at the beach.
  • Dinner (Condo): Taco Night!
☕
Daily Treat: Kai Coffee (Macadamia Nut Latte).
Book Diamond Head Hike
Day 5 (Feb 13): Free Day in Oahu Flex Day
📅 The Plan
  • Use this to relax by the condo pool, rent surfboards in Waikiki, or do souvenir shopping.

🌋 PART 2: BIG ISLAND (The Road Trip)

Transport: Rental Minivan required. Stay: Waikoloa/Kohala Coast Condo.

Day 6 (Feb 14): Island Hop Travel
📅 The Plan
  • 11:00 AM: Check out of the Waikiki Banyan.
  • Fly HNL → KOA. Depart 1:18 PM, Arrive 2:06 PM.
  • Pick up Minivan.
  • Immediate Stop: Costco Kona (Near Airport) to stock the condo.
  • Check into Condo (Waikoloa).
🍳 Menu
  • Dinner: "Arrival Feast" (Rotisserie Chicken, Bagged Caesar Salad, Rice).
🍦
Daily Treat: Tropical Dreams Ice Cream (Buy a pint at the grocery store).
Day 7 (Feb 15): The Grand Loop (Volcanoes) National Park
📅 The Plan
  • Early start (7:30 AM). Drive the scenic Southern Route (Highway 11) from Waikoloa.
  • Morning Stop: Punaluʻu Black Sand Beach. Walk the jet-black sand and look for resting sea turtles.
  • Afternoon: Hawaiʻi Volcanoes National Park. Hike the Kīlauea Iki Trail.
  • Evening Drive: Return via Saddle Road after dinner.
🍳 Menu
  • Lunch (Packed): Sandwiches eaten right on the beach at Punaluʻu.
  • Dinner (Cheap Eats): Cafe 100 in Hilo.
🍪
Daily Treat: Big Island Candies (Hilo).
National Park Info ($30/car)
Day 8 (Feb 16): Best Beach & Stargazing Relax + Space
📅 The Plan
  • Morning: Hapuna Beach State Recreation Area.
  • Evening: Mauna Kea Visitor Center (Stargazing).
🍳 Menu
  • Lunch (Packed): Wraps & Chips (at Beach).
  • Dinner (Condo): Leftover Rotisserie Chicken Fried Rice.
🍧
Daily Treat: Original Big Island Shave Ice Co.
Day 9 (Feb 17): History & North Tip Culture + Adventure
📅 The Plan
🏛️ Added Stop: Puʻukoholā Heiau NHS
Stop here first. Time: 45 mins. Cost: Free.

Then, choose your Adventure:

Option A: Ziplining (Kohala)
  • Best for: Adrenaline lovers. (Approx $200/person).

  • Lunch (Packed): Picnic at the beach.

Daily Treat: Tex Drive-In (Hot Malasadas on the drive back).
Day 10 (Feb 18): The Long Goodbye Red-Eye Departure
📅 The Plan
  • Check out of Condo (ask for late checkout if possible).
  • Spend the afternoon exploring Kailua-Kona town, getting souvenirs, or a final beach dip.
  • Head to KOA airport for your 10:30 PM flight.
🍳 Menu
  • Lunch: Final Poke Bowl or Food Truck.
  • Dinner: Grab a nice sit-down dinner in Kona town before heading to the airport.
