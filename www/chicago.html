🗓️ Daily Itinerary

Day 0: Saturday, June 20 — The Road to Madison
Morning Depart Sioux Falls for the 6-hour drive.
3:00 PM Check-in: Residence Inn Madison West/Middleton.
Evening Relax by the simple fun pool and reorganize cooler backpacks for Chicago.
Day 1: Sunday, June 21 — River, Sky & Rails
1:30 PM Ping Tom Park: 15-min walk from VRBO for playground time.
2:15 PM Water Taxi: Chinatown to Michigan Ave.
3:00 PM Cloud Gate: 10-min walk south for "The Bean" photos.
4:30 PM Dinner: Chicago's Dog House: Quick-service Chicago Dogs at North Bridge (Level 4).
6:00 PM 360 CHICAGO: Sunset and TILT experience.
8:30 PM Return: Take Red Line South (Chicago Ave station) to Cermak-Chinatown.
Day 2: Monday, June 22 — Aquatic Morning & Museum Preview
08:30 AM Transit: Take Northbound #146 Bus from State & Cermak to Museum Campus.
09:00 AM Shedd Aquarium: Explore Abbott Oceanarium and Polar Play Zone.
12:30 PM Picnic: Lunch at "Man with a Fish" statue (outdoor skyline view).
2:00 PM Field Museum: Preview using ASTC reciprocity for Pokémon Exhibit.
4:30 PM Return Transit: Take Southbound #146 Bus from Museum Campus back to State & Cermak.
Day 3: Tuesday, June 23 — Science & Industry Full Day
09:02 AM Transit: Take Metra Electric (ME) Southbound from McCormick Place station.
09:10 AM Arrival: Arrive at 55th-56th-57th St Station; 8-min walk to museum entrance.
09:30 AM Griffin Museum of Science and Industry: Enter at opening. Prioritize the Idea Factory (Ages 10 & under) and the U-505 Submarine.
12:30 PM Packer Lunch: Eat in the Siragusa Center (Lower Level), a dedicated space for families with their own food.
4:30 PM Return: Catch Northbound Metra Electric back to McCormick Place for a relaxing evening at the VRBO.
Day 4: Wednesday, June 24 — History & Navy Pier Fireworks
10:00 AM Transit: #146 Bus to Field Museum for final exploration.
2:30 PM Optional: Walk to Adler Planetarium.
4:30 PM Shoreline Water Taxi: Lake Route from Museum Campus (near Adler) to Navy Pier.
5:30 PM Dinner: Giordano's on Navy Pier (Order deep dish via app 1 hour early!).
9:00 PM Fireworks: Summer display over Lake Michigan.
10:00 PM Return Transit: Take #29 Bus South from Navy Pier to State & Cermak.
Day 5: Thursday, June 25 — The Bean & Return Drive
09:00 AM Check-out: Depart VRBO and drive north to Millennium Park.
09:30 AM Parking: Park at Millennium Park Garage or Lakeside Garage.
10:00 AM Millennium Park: Photos at "The Bean" (Cloud Gate) and a final run at Maggie Daley Park.
11:30 AM Departure: Begin the 9-hour drive back to Sioux Falls.
