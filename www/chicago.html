🏙️ Chicago: Museums, Skyline & Deep Dish

Travelers: Family of 6 Dates: June 20 – June 25, 2026

✅ Pre-Trip To-Do List

  • MEMBERSHIP: Bring Washington Pavilion Card (ASTC Logo) for Field Museum reciprocity.
  • PACKING: Prep cooler backpacks with sandwiches and water for museum days.
  • TRANSIT: Bookmark the #146 Bus Schedule and Water Taxi Schedule.

  • 2 large bunches of Bananas
  • Assorted fresh fruit (berries/grapes)

  • 3 loaves of sliced Bread & 1-2 packs of Wraps
  • 3 lbs sliced Deli Meat (Turkey/Ham)
  • 2 packs of sliced Cheese
  • Squeeze bottles of Mayo & Mustard
  • 1 large variety box of Chips
  • 1 bag of Baby Carrots + Ranch dip
  • Fruit cups or applesauce pouches
  • 2 twenty-four packs of Bottled Water

🗓️ Daily Itinerary

Day 0: Saturday, June 20 — The Road to Madison
09:00 AM Depart Sioux Falls for Madison, WI.
12:30 PM Car Lunch: Simple handheld sandwiches eaten on the road inside the van.
3:00 PM Check-in: Residence Inn Madison West/Middleton.
Evening Relax by the simple fun pool. Dinner out near hotel/takeaway.
Day 1: Sunday, June 21 — Travel & Relaxing at VRBO Taco Night
08:00 AM Breakfast: Free breakfast at hotel before driving to Chicago.
12:30 PM Car Lunch: Quick mid-drive car snacks/wraps to stay on schedule.
Afternoon Hang Out: Arrive in Chicago, check into the VRBO, play games, and relax. Ping Tom Park is a short 15-min walk away if the kids need playground time.
5:30 PM Dinner: Taco Night at the VRBO! Cook up a family taco feast and enjoy a relaxing evening.
Day 2: Monday, June 22 — Shedd, Field Museum & Navy Pier Giordano's
07:45 AM Breakfast: Cereal, bananas, and fresh fruit at the VRBO.
08:30 AM Transit: Take Northbound #146 Bus from State & Cermak to Museum Campus.
09:00 AM Shedd Aquarium: Explore Abbott Oceanarium and Polar Play Zone.
12:30 PM Picnic Lunch: Outside at "Man with a Fish" statue (skyline views from cooler backpacks).
2:00 PM Field Museum: Preview using ASTC reciprocity for Pokémon Exhibit.
4:30 PM Shoreline Water Taxi: Lake Route from Museum Campus (near Adler) to Navy Pier (alternatively, take #146 Bus).
5:30 PM Dinner: Giordano's on Navy Pier: Famous deep dish pizza (order via app 1 hour early!).
7:30 PM Return Transit: Take #29 Bus South from Navy Pier back to State & Cermak.
Day 3: Tuesday, June 23 — Science & Industry Full Day
08:00 AM Breakfast: Cereal, bananas, and fresh fruit at the VRBO.
09:02 AM Transit: Take Metra Electric (ME) Southbound from McCormick Place station.
09:10 AM Arrival: Arrive at 55th-56th-57th St Station; 8-min walk to museum entrance.
09:30 AM Griffin Museum of Science and Industry: Enter at opening. Prioritize the Idea Factory and U-505 Submarine.
12:30 PM Packer Lunch: Eat in the Siragusa Center (Lower Level), a dedicated space for families with their own food.
4:30 PM Return: Catch Northbound Metra Electric back to McCormick Place.
5:30 PM Dinner: Family night in; cook baked pasta/steak burgers at the VRBO.
Day 4: Wednesday, June 24 — Museums, Chicago Dogs & 360 Chicago Fireworks
08:30 AM Breakfast: Cereal, bananas, and fresh fruit at the VRBO.
10:00 AM Transit: Take Northbound #146 Bus from State & Cermak to Museum Campus.
10:30 AM Museum Time: Return to the Field Museum for final exploration or visit the Adler Planetarium.
12:30 PM Picnic Lunch: Pack-and-eat lunch on the Museum Campus lawns from cooler backpacks.
3:45 PM Transit to North Bridge: Take Northbound #146 Bus to North Bridge / Grand Ave.
4:30 PM Early Dinner: Enjoy Chicago Dogs at Chicago's Dog House: (Level 4, North Bridge).
6:00 PM Walk to 360: Walk north along Michigan Ave to the John Hancock Building (~10 mins).
6:30 PM 360 CHICAGO: Head up for the TILT experience, sunset, and watch the Navy Pier Fireworks (starting at 9:00 PM) from high above!
9:30 PM Return Transit: Take Red Line South (Chicago Ave station) to Cermak-Chinatown.
Day 5: Thursday, June 25 — The Bean & Return Drive
08:00 AM Breakfast: Final VRBO breakfast (cereal, remaining bananas/fruit, clean out fridge).
09:00 AM Check-out: Depart VRBO and drive north to Millennium Park.
09:30 AM Parking: Park at Millennium Park Garage or Lakeside Garage.
10:00 AM Millennium Park: Photos at "The Bean" (Cloud Gate) and a final run at Maggie Daley Park.
11:10 AM Car Lunch: Prep and eat packed deli sandwiches inside the van right before hitting the road.
11:30 AM Departure: Begin the 9-hour drive back to Sioux Falls.
06:00 PM Road Dinner: Fast-food drive-thru stop along I-90 in Albert Lea, MN to break up the drive.
