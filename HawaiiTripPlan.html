🌺 Hawaii 2027: Family Adventure Plan

Travelers: Family of 6 (Kids: 16, 13, 10, 7)
Theme: "Grocery-First" Adventure with Bonus Options
Strategy: Mix of free culture, nature, and targeted paid activities.
Estimated Budget: $14,000 – $17,500

🌋 PART 1: BIG ISLAND (The Road Trip)

Transport: Rental Minivan required. Stay: Waikoloa/Kohala Coast Condo.

Day 1: Landing & Logistics Arrival
📅 The Plan
  • Land at KOA Airport. Pick up Minivan.
  • Immediate Stop: Costco Kona (Near Airport).
  • Check into Condo (Waikoloa).
  • Sunset swim at Anaehoʻomalu Bay (A-Bay).
  • Dinner: "Arrival Feast" at condo.
🍳 Menu
  • Dinner: Rotisserie Chicken, Bagged Caesar Salad, Rice, Pineapple.
🍦
Daily Treat: Tropical Dreams Ice Cream (Buy a pint at the grocery store).
Day 2: Volcanoes National Park National Park
📅 The Plan
  • Early start (7:30 AM). Drive Saddle Road to Hilo side.
  • Hike: Kīlauea Iki Trail.
  • Walk: Thurston Lava Tube.
🍳 Menu
  • Lunch (Packed): Sandwiches (Steam Vents picnic area).
  • Dinner (Cheap Eats): Cafe 100 in Hilo.
🍪
Daily Treat: Big Island Candies.
Day 3: Best Beach & Stargazing Relax + Space
📅 The Plan
  • Morning: Hapuna Beach State Park.
  • Evening: Mauna Kea Visitor Center (Stargazing).
🍳 Menu
  • Lunch (Packed): Wraps & Chips (at Beach).
  • Dinner (Condo): Leftover Rotisserie Chicken Fried Rice.
🍧
Daily Treat: Original Big Island Shave Ice Co.
Day 4: History & North Tip Culture + Adventure
📅 The Plan
🏛️ Added Stop: Puʻukoholā Heiau NHS
Stop here first (it's on the way North).
Time: 45 mins. Cost: Free.

Then, continue North to your Adventure Choice:

Option A: Ziplining (Kohala)
  • Best for: Adrenaline lovers.
  • Cost: Approx $200/person.
Option B: Pololū Valley Hike
  • Best for: Nature lovers & Budget saving.
  • Activity: Hike to black sand beach.
🍳 Menu
  • Lunch (Packed): Picnic at the beach.
  • Dinner (Condo): Taco Night!
🍩
Daily Treat: Tex Drive-In (Malasadas).

🛒 Big Island Costco List (Day 1 - Kona)

Main Meals
2 Rotisserie Chickens Dinner Day 1 + Leftovers for Day 3 Fried Rice.
Ground Beef (3-4 lbs) For Taco Night (Day 4).
Bagged Salads (2 big kits) Vegetable side for chicken.
Rice (5lb bag) Dinner Day 1 + Fried Rice Day 3.
Taco Shells / Tortillas Taco Night.
Breakfast & Lunch
Eggs (24 pack) & Bacon Hearty breakfasts.
Bagels / Bread (2 packs) Breakfasts & Sandwiches.
Deli Meat & Cheese Sandwiches for 4 days.
Tropical Fruit Pineapple/Papaya.
Pantry & Essentials (Don't Forget!)
Cooking Oil / Butter Required for eggs, frying rice, and browning meat!
Mayonnaise / Mustard Sandwiches are dry without these.
Soy Sauce (Aloha Shoyu) Essential for the Day 3 Fried Rice.
Coffee & Creamer Rental condos often provide terrible coffee.
Ziploc Bags (Variety Pack) Mandatory for packing lunches/snacks.
Case of Water Refill bottles for hiking.

🌴 PART 2: OAHU (City & Surf)

Transport: Uber/Bus + 1 Day Rental. Stay: Waikiki Condo.

Day 5: Transfer & Waikiki City Life
🍳 Menu
  • Dinner: Marukame Udon (Eating Out).
🍍
Daily Treat: Dole Whip (Royal Hawaiian Center).
Day 6: Pearl Harbor History
📅 The Plan
  • Core: USS Arizona Memorial.
  • Bonus: Battleship Missouri ($$).
  • Evening: Free Hula Show at Kūhiō Beach.
🍳 Menu
  • Lunch: Packed Snacks/Sandwiches.
  • Dinner (Condo): Spaghetti Night.
🥞
Daily Treat: Leonard's Bakery Malasadas.
Day 7: North Shore Loop 1-Day Car Rental
📅 The Plan
  • Morning Option: Shark's Cove (Snorkel) OR Waimea Valley (Walk).
  • Afternoon: Dole Plantation.
  • Scenic Drive: Optional return via East Coast (Blowhole).
🍳 Menu
  • Lunch: Food Trucks (Eating Out).
  • Dinner: Leftovers.
🍧
Daily Treat: Matsumoto Shave Ice.
Day 8: Hike & Fly Departure
📅 The Plan
  • Hike Diamond Head. Final swim. Fly home.

🛒 Oahu Grocery List (Target / Safeway / Instacart)

Meals & Snacks

Ground Beef (1 lb) For Spaghetti sauce (optional but better).
Milk & Cereal Fast breakfasts before the Bus.
Bread & PBJ/Deli Meat Lunches (if not eating leftovers).
